See the CSRF write-up for how it works and why it is served from this project instead of a second domain.

What that button actually does

It is a plain link to /vulnerable/logout.php. If you were signed in to the vulnerable site in this browser, clicking it just logged you out with no confirmation, then landed you here or on the login page. Nothing about this page could tell your browser was signed in anywhere; it only needed you to click a link that pointed at someone else's site.

← Sign back in to the vulnerable site and click the button again to see it a second time.

What loaded the moment this page opened

A script on this page also sent a request to the vulnerable site's "save post" endpoint the instant you arrived, using whatever vulnerable-site session cookie your browser already had. No click needed.
